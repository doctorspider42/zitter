! Description: Ad and analytics libraries that the other lists block get a
! harmless stand-in (adblock_resources.js) instead of a failed request, so a
! page that checks whether they loaded does not see the blocking. Nothing is
! fetched from the network. `redirect-rule` acts only on requests another
! rule already blocks: an exception or a site switched off still wins.
! See docs/ad-blocking.md, "Built-in lists".
!
